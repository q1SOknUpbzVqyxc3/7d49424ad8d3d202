Ironvane Pass

How it works

Four steps, no surprises in the middle.

None of them starts before the previous one closes.

01

Application

You pick a tier; we look at your experience, volumes and sources. If the tier is the wrong size for you, we say so and point at the right one.

02

Deposit

You place the month's fixed sum. It opens the tier's sessions and shows you can carry the volume you claimed.

03

Sessions

You get an offer, a geo and a bundle for each session, and run it to the plan agreed at the start of the month.

04

Settlement

We total net profit for the month, take 20%, and return the deposit in full on 100% of sessions completed.

The deposit

Why a deposit and not a fee.

The deposit does not buy access. It shows you can carry the tier's volume. It returns in full at the end of the month when every session is done. We earn on the percentage of your profit — and on nothing else.

  • Returned on 100% of sessions completed within the calendar month
  • A session counts as completed against the plan agreed at the start of the month
  • Unfinished sessions do not roll into the next month
  • The percentage is calculated after the deposit is returned, on net profit

Invite code

Bringing in your own.

The invite code unlocks at GOLD, after a month in the team with 100% of sessions completed. One code, one guest: they run two trial sessions with no deposit and decide afterwards whether to take a tier.

  • One code, one participant
  • Codes are not for sale or transfer to third parties
  • A code can only be activated inside its validity window
  • Trial sessions run in full format, nothing trimmed
